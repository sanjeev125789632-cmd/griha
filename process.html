Architectural Design Process in Prayagraj

Every building project at Griha Architects progresses through a structured five-stage delivery roadmap. From initial consultation to final key handover, our milestone framework eliminates construction delays, budget overruns, and compliance surprises.

Structured 5-Phase Project Delivery

  1. 1

    Consultation & Site Feasibility

    We begin with a thorough discussion of your spatial requirements, aesthetic preferences, budget boundaries, and timeline expectations. Our team conducts an on-site survey in Prayagraj to measure physical dimensions, assess soil and drainage conditions, verify road width, and cross-reference municipal setback requirements under PDA zoning rules.

    Key Deliverables: Site measurement docket, orientation analysis (sun path and prevailing wind), and preliminary project charter.
  2. 2

    Design: 2D Spatial Planning & 3D Photorealism

    Our architects transform conceptual briefs into precise 2D floor plans that optimize circulation, daylighting, and spatial efficiency. Once floor plans are approved by the client, we develop detailed 3D exterior elevations and interior renderings showing exact material finishes, textures, window framing, and false ceiling lighting details.

    Key Deliverables: Dimensioned 2D floor maps, furniture layout plans, exterior 3D perspectives, and initial material palette boards.
  3. 3

    Approvals & Structural Working Drawings

    We prepare statutory submission packages aligned with Prayagraj Development Authority (PDA) and municipal bylaws. Simultaneously, our structural engineers calculate foundation depths, column reinforcements, and RCC slab schedules to guarantee seismic resilience and load compliance before any excavation begins.

    Key Deliverables: PDA sanction drawings, structural calculations, column center-line plans, and complete MEP (plumbing & electrical) schematics.
  4. 4

    Execution: Turnkey Construction & Site Supervision

    Execution commences under direct architect supervision. For turnkey projects, our firm manages material procurement, subcontractor scheduling, steel reinforcement audits, concrete curing checks, and modular carpentry fabrication to ensure complete fidelity to architectural drawings.

    Key Deliverables: Milestone progress updates, material quality inspection reports, and on-site snag resolution.
  5. 5

    Handover & Completion Documentation

    Prior to occupancy, our team conducts a rigorous snagging inspection covering electrical load balancing, sanitary waterproofing, modular cabinet alignments, and paint finishes. Once all punch-list items are resolved, the completed building or interior is formally handed over to the client with maintenance guidance.

    Key Deliverables: As-built drawings docket, warranty certifications for modular hardware, and municipal completion assistance.
