Leadership & Architectural Practice

Ar. Chandan Singh: Principal Architect in Prayagraj

Leading Griha Architects with a design philosophy rooted in functional space planning, structural longevity, and contextual design tailored to the climate and urban fabric of Prayagraj.

Ar. Chandan Singh (Chandan Singh Bhartiya) founded Griha Architects to bridge the divide between theoretical architectural concepts and practical on-site construction reality in Uttar Pradesh. Having overseen diverse residential layouts, commercial office complexes, and interior spaces, Ar. Chandan Singh provides direct hands-on oversight to every project undertaken by the practice.

His architectural methodology balances contemporary aesthetic sensibilities with traditional spatial wisdom, including Vastu Shastra principles where requested by clients, alongside rigorous cross-ventilation, daylight optimization, and structural safety standards.

About Griha Architects: Civil Lines, Prayagraj

Griha Architects operates from its central studio at Vinayak Triveni Tower on the Kanpur–Allahabad Highway in Civil Lines, Prayagraj. We are a specialized multi-disciplinary practice bringing together architectural drafting, interior design, and turnkey general contracting.

PDA Bylaws & Municipal Compliance

Navigating building codes and setback regulations in Prayagraj requires technical precision. We produce comprehensive submission packages aligned with Prayagraj Development Authority (PDA) norms to ensure smooth map sanctions.

Integrated Interior & Modular Craft

Unlike practices that outsource interior carpentry to fragmented third parties, Griha Architects plans modular kitchens, wardrobes, and false ceilings simultaneously with civil drawings for seamless electrical and plumbing alignment.

Turnkey Execution with On-Site Supervision

We eliminate contractor misunderstandings by providing unified design-build management. From structural foundation excavation to final painting and sanitary fittings, our architect-led supervision guarantees quality control.

Client Confirmations & Credential Transparency

Griha Architects maintains strict transparency in all professional representations. Client testimonials, project cost breakdowns, and formal council accreditation records are updated as verified documentation is compiled.
