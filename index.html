Do you handle Prayagraj municipal house map approvals (PDA / Nagar Nigam)?

Yes. Griha Architects coordinates full 2D submission drawings, structural verification, and municipal permit coordination in compliance with Prayagraj Development Authority (PDA) building bylaws and zoning regulations.

What is the typical timeline for an architectural design or 3D floor plan in Prayagraj?
